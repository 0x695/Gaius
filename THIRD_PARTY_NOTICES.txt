Miles Audio Interface Library 2.14 -- the music driver  (every build)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The code that turns the game's music files into FM notes (audio/ail_xmidi.cpp)
follows the source of the Audio Interface Library driver the original game shipped
(XMIDI.ASM and YAMAHA.INC, release 2.14 of 14 November 1992). John Miles released
that source to the public as freeware in 2000, "usable by anyone for any purpose".
