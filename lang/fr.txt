Skip tutorial = Passer le tutoriel
Welcome, governor = Bienvenue, gouverneur
Rome has given you a province. Build its capital, keep the peace, and Rome will promote you. = Rome te confie une province. Batis sa capitale, garde la paix, et Rome te donnera de l'avancement.
Rome judges four ratings - Peace, Culture, Prosperity and Empire. To rise from Citizen to Equitus each must reach 12 and their average 35. = Rome juge quatre notes - Paix, Culture, Prosperite et Empire. Pour passer de Citizen a Equitus, chacune doit atteindre 12 et leur moyenne 35.
This tutorial leads you there. A gold frame marks the button to press next. = Ce tutoriel t'y mene. Un cadre dore montre le bouton a presser.
Build a Forum = Batis un Forum
A Forum = Un Forum
A city begins with its Forum. It gathers the taxes of the houses around it and sends its citizens along the roads. = Une ville commence par son Forum. Il percoit l'impot des maisons alentour et envoie ses citoyens le long des routes.
Find open land near water, choose Forum, take the first type (Aventine, 60 Dn) and place it. = Trouve un terrain libre pres de l'eau, choisis Forum, prends le premier type (Aventine, 60 Dn) et pose-le.
The arrow keys scroll the map, and so does the pointer at the screen's edge. The right button puts a tool away. = Les fleches font defiler la carte, comme le pointeur au bord de l'ecran. Le bouton droit range un outil.
Drag a finger to scroll the map. Tap the map to see what would be built there, tap it again to build. = Glisse un doigt pour faire defiler la carte. Touche la carte pour voir ce qui serait bati, touche encore pour batir.
Lay 12 cells of road = Pose 12 cases de route
Houses grow only where the Forum's citizens pass, and those keep to the roads. = Les maisons ne grandissent que la ou passent les citoyens du Forum, et ils suivent les routes.
Choose Road and drag it from beside the Forum, some twenty cells. Leave room on both sides for houses. = Choisis Road et tire-la depuis le cote du Forum, sur une vingtaine de cases. Laisse de la place des deux cotes pour les maisons.
The right button during a drag takes it back. Clear Area removes what was built by mistake. = Le bouton droit pendant le trace l'annule. Clear Area enleve ce qui a ete bati par erreur.
Drag a finger to lay it. Undo takes the last drag back. Clear Area removes what was built by mistake. = Glisse un doigt pour la poser. Annuler reprend le dernier trace. Clear Area enleve ce qui a ete bati par erreur.
Place 12 houses = Pose 12 maisons
Choose Housing and place a dozen plots along the road, within six cells of the Forum - further out they pay no tax. = Choisis Housing et pose une douzaine de parcelles le long de la route, a six cases du Forum au plus - plus loin elles ne paient pas d'impot.
Each plot starts as a tent. Where life is good it grows, step by step, into a villa that holds more people and pays more tax. Where it is not, the tent folds. = Chaque parcelle commence par une tente. La ou la vie est bonne elle grandit, pas a pas, jusqu'a la villa qui loge plus de monde et paie plus d'impot. Sinon la tente disparait.
Dig 3 wells = Creuse 3 puits
No house grows past a pair of tents without water. = Sans eau, aucune maison ne depasse deux tentes.
Choose Well and dig one among the houses. A well serves only the plots that touch it, so dig one for every few houses. = Choisis Well et creuses-en un parmi les maisons. Un puits ne sert que les parcelles qui le touchent - creuses-en un toutes les quelques maisons.
Later a reservoir on the water's edge, a pipe and a Fountain will water a whole district. = Plus tard un reservoir au bord de l'eau, un tuyau et une Fountain arroseront tout un quartier.
Build a Market and 2 Workshops = Un Market et 2 Workshops
Work = Travail
People need work. Where many are idle, the houses decay. = Il faut du travail. La ou beaucoup sont oisifs, les maisons deperissent.
Build a Market beside the road among the houses. Then two Workshops at the edge of the houses - they want people living within three cells, and a Market along their road. = Batis un Market au bord de la route parmi les maisons. Puis deux Workshops en bordure des maisons - il leur faut des habitants a trois cases au plus et un Market sur leur route.
Choose different goods for each. The Forum's industry advisor knows which suit this province. = Choisis un produit different pour chacun. Le conseiller a l'industrie du Forum sait lesquels conviennent a la province.
Build Bath Houses = Batis des Bath Houses
Bath Houses = Les bains
Houses ask for more as they grow - water, then a market, then baths, later a school and a theater. = En grandissant les maisons demandent plus - l'eau, puis un marche, puis des bains, plus tard une ecole et un theatre.
Choose Bath Houses and build them beside the road among the houses. They serve the plots within three cells. = Choisis Bath Houses et batis-les au bord de la route parmi les maisons. Ils servent les parcelles a trois cases au plus.
Visit the Forum = Visite le Forum
Your advisors = Tes conseillers
Go to Forum takes you to your advisors. The Treasurer keeps the accounts, and the people's tax can rise from 5 to 6 percent at no cost. = Go to Forum mene a tes conseillers. Le Tresorier tient les comptes, et l'impot par tete peut passer de 5 a 6 pour cent sans dommage.
At the year's end Rome takes a tribute, and most of the year's profit if more than 500 Dn lie in the treasury. Miss three tributes and you are dismissed. = En fin d'annee Rome prend un tribut, et l'essentiel du benefice s'il reste plus de 500 Dn en caisse. Trois tributs manques et tu es renvoye.
The Tribune's plebs fight fires and mend buildings and roads. He staffs them by himself. = Les plebeiens du Tribun combattent le feu et entretiennent batiments et routes. Il les repartit lui-meme.
The Tribune's plebs fight fires and mend buildings and roads. Give each duty the number in brackets. = Les plebeiens du Tribun combattent le feu et entretiennent batiments et routes. Donne a chaque tache le nombre entre parentheses.
Grow to 2000 people = Atteins 2000 habitants
A bigger city = Une ville plus grande
Taxes are your income, and Prosperity and Culture rise no higher than the city's size allows. Rome wants 2000 people at the least. = L'impot est ton revenu, et Prosperite et Culture ne montent pas plus haut que la taille de la ville. Rome veut au moins 2000 habitants.
Add houses along new roads, with water, a market and baths in reach, and work for the newcomers - a Workshop employs 80 people, a Market 50, a Forum 120. = Ajoute des maisons le long de nouvelles routes, avec eau, marche et bains a portee, et du travail pour les arrivants - un Workshop emploie 80 personnes, un Market 50, un Forum 120.
A second Forum further along carries the taxes and the citizens to new streets. = Un second Forum plus loin sert de nouvelles rues.
Build an Oracle = Batis un Oracle
Culture is counted per head - temples, oracles, schools and shows for the people you have. = La Culture se compte par tete - temples, oracles, ecoles et spectacles pour les habitants que tu as.
An Oracle (200 Dn) is the quickest way. One is enough for about 2000 people, so build more as the city grows. = Un Oracle (200 Dn) est le plus rapide. Un seul suffit pour 2000 habitants environ - batis-en d'autres quand la ville grandit.
Temples are cheap, calm the streets and add a little. = Les temples coutent peu, calment les rues et ajoutent un peu.
Link the Imperial Highway = Relie l'Imperial Highway
The province = La province
Go to Province shows the land you govern - your city, four towns and, at one edge, the junction of the Imperial Highway, marked in gold. = Go to Province montre tes terres - ta ville, quatre villages et, sur un bord, le depart de l'Imperial Highway, marque en or.
Choose Highway and drag it from the junction to the city (50 Dn a cell, more on rough ground). It is worth 20 to your Empire rating, and straight roads add more. = Choisis Highway et tire-la du depart jusqu'a la ville (50 Dn la case, plus en terrain difficile). Elle vaut 20 pour la note Empire, et les routes droites ajoutent encore.
Should a piece of road wear away, lay it again - a gap breaks the link. = Si un morceau de route s'efface, repose-le - un trou coupe la liaison.
Link 2 towns by road = Relie 2 villages par la route
The towns = Les villages
A town joined to your city by road trades with it and grows. Each is worth up to 20 to your Empire rating. = Un village relie a ta ville par la route commerce et grandit. Chacun vaut jusqu'a 20 pour la note Empire.
Choose Road (30 Dn a cell) and drag it from a town marked in gold to the city - or just across the Highway, which a road that crosses it shares. Link two now and the others as the funds allow. = Choisis Road (30 Dn la case) et tire-la d'un village dore jusqu'a la ville - ou juste en travers de la Highway, qu'une route qui la croise emprunte. Relies-en deux, puis les autres.
The standard beside the city is your Cohort. Cohort Patrol sends it out when barbarians are sighted. = L'etendard pres de la ville est ta Cohorte. Cohort Patrol l'envoie quand des barbares sont signales.
Earn Rome's promotion = Merite la promotion de Rome
In brackets, what an Equitus needs. A year is in profit only if you spend less than you earn, building included. Once the city stands, let the years pass and accept the promotion. = Entre parentheses, ce qu'il faut a un Equitus. Une annee n'est en benefice que si tu depenses moins que tu ne gagnes, constructions comprises. La ville faite, laisse passer les annees et accepte la promotion.
Well done, Equitus = Bravo, Equitus
Rome has promoted you. A new province and an empty site for its capital are yours, and all you have learned applies again. = Rome t'a promu. Une nouvelle province et un site vide pour sa capitale sont a toi, et tout ce que tu as appris vaut de nouveau.
From now on Rome asks more with every rank and the barbarians come sooner. Your advisors in the Forum know where you stand. = Desormais Rome demande plus a chaque rang et les barbares viennent plus tot. Tes conseillers au Forum savent ou tu en es.
Good luck, governor! = Bonne chance, gouverneur !
